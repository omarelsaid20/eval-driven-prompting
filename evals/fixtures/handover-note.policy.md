Escalation policy (handbook chapter 4)

4.1 Who decides. Reply Assist never approves money, replacements or cancellations. A ticket it escalates goes to a support colleague. That colleague decides, or passes the ticket on as below. Nothing is decided until a colleague records it on the order.

4.2 Damaged or faulty items. The customer sends a photo or video first. Refund or replacement up to 100 EUR (or 100 GBP): the support colleague decides. Above that, or for compensation (money without a return), a team lead approves.

4.3 Lost or stalled parcels. If tracking has not moved for 7 days, the colleague opens a trace with the carrier. A refund or a resend waits on the trace result or on 14 days without a scan.

4.4 Payments. Double charges, missing refunds and refunds pending finance go to the finance team. Support does not reverse payments.

4.5 Changes after shipping. Address changes and cancellations after the order has shipped go through the carrier. Before shipping, the colleague can cancel in the order system.

4.6 Repeat contacts. A customer who asks for a manager, or writes a third time about one issue, is flagged to the team lead on the same day.
